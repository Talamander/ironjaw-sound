# Third-party notices

Ironjaw Sound is a fictional map. Its terrain is built from public elevation and bathymetry data of the Prince
William Sound area in Alaska, then reshaped (relief compressed, coastline and valleys edited, new names). It is not
a real-world map and must not be used for navigation.

## Data in the map

- Elevation: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program 1 arc-second DEM (public domain), modified. Use of this
  data does not imply endorsement by the USGS.
- Bathymetry: NOAA National Geophysical Data Center. 2009: Prince William Sound, Alaska 8/3 Arc-second MHHW
  Coastal Digital Elevation Model. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. (U.S. Government work, not
  subject to copyright in the U.S.), modified. Not for navigation.
- Bathymetry: NOAA National Geophysical Data Center. 2009: Prince William Sound, Alaska 8 Arc-second MHHW Coastal
  Digital Elevation Model. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. (U.S. Government work), modified.
  Not for navigation.
- Bathymetry: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. 2020. Cordova, Alaska 8/15 arc-second Coastal
  Digital Elevation Model. (U.S. Government work), modified. Not for navigation.
- Bathymetry: NOAA National Geophysical Data Center. 2009: Whittier, Alaska 8/15 arc-second MHHW Coastal Digital
  Elevation Model. NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. (U.S. Government work), modified. Not for
  navigation.
- Bathymetry: NOAA National Geophysical Data Center. 2009: Southern Alaska Coastal Relief Model. NOAA National
  Centers for Environmental Information. https://doi.org/10.7289/V58G8HMQ (U.S. Government work), modified. Not
  for navigation.

## Made for this map

The terrain textures, snow spruce trees, rocks, roads, runways and missions are original work for Ironjaw Sound,
generated with our own scripts and headless Blender. No asset is extracted from Nuclear Option or another game.

## Unity

The map bundle was built with Unity 2022.3 and contains compiled Universal Render Pipeline shaders. Unity and URP
shader code are covered by the Unity Companion License (https://unity.com/legal/licenses/unity-companion-license).

## Nuclear Option content used at runtime

TerrainLabGameplay places copies of the game's own buildings (houses, apartment and office blocks, industrial sheds, wind turbines and power pylons) on
the map while you play. It copies them from your installed game. Nothing from the game is included in this
package.
